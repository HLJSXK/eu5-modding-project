# Aragonese Empire (CDA) — Custom buildings
# National Destinies mod

# Distinct from vanilla building llotja_seda (Valencia silk exchange,
# event_only_buildings.txt). Our buildings represent the Catalan-Aragonese

# royal arsenal of Barcelona, and the Catalan consulate trade network.
