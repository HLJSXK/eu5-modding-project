# === Cilician Armenia Advance Tree ===
# Stepping-stone destination (BYZ/RUM/ROM available regionally) -> 4-5 advances per age.
# Vanilla armenian + miaphysite advances are dense (9+31), so we focus on the
# Mediterranean/maritime/Hethumid/Lusignan story that vanilla does not cover:
# Italian trade concessions, Mongol diplomacy, Cilician knightly culture, port
# emporia at Ayas/Korikos, the Catholicate's western migration.
