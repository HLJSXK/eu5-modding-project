# 4. Watandari & Kulkarni Registry -- the Maratha state inherited from its
#    Yadava and Bahmani predecessors a dense layer of hereditary village
#    officeholders (watandars). The patil was the village headman, the

# 5. Chauth and Sardeshmukhi -- the twin tribute claims that defined Maratha
#    expansion. The chauth was a 25% levy on revenue collected from territories
#    the Marathas claimed protection over, while the sardeshmukhi added a
#    further 10% as the "chief headman's right." Both rights were extracted
#    by treaty from the dying Mughal empire after Bahadur Shah's death and
#    formalized by Shahu after 1719. They funded the Confederacy's expansion
#    deep into north India but cast every tributary state as a temporary
#    revenue source rather than an integrated province, and earned the
