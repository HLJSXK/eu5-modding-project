# National Destinies -- Anglo-French Dominion (CAN) bureaucracies.
# Six historically-grounded institutions of the British North American
# settlement that became Canada: the Quebec Act of 1774 by which George
# III's parliament permanently preserved the seigneurial regime, French
# civil law, and Catholic establishment of the conquered St. Lawrence
# colony; the Hudson's Bay Company chartered by Charles II in 1670 as
# "the Governor and Company of Adventurers of England trading into
# Hudson's Bay" and reorganised in 1821 after absorbing the Montreal
# Northwest Company; the Family Compact / Chateau Clique appointed
# oligarchies that ran Upper and Lower Canada through the Legislative
# Council and the Anglican-Tory and seigneurial-merchant networks
# behind it (1791-1841); the seigneurial system of New France preserved
# in the Article 4 capitulation of 1760 and finally abolished in 1854;
# the Captain of Militia (capitaine de milice), the parish-level habitant
# officer the British retained from the French regime to relay royal
# proclamations, requisition corvee labour, and lead local militia; and
# the elected colonial assemblies of the Constitutional Act of 1791,
# the first popularly-elected parliaments north of the Floridas.
#
# Auto-modifier grants 2 slots; capital adds 1 = 3 effective. Player
# picks 3 of 6. All IDs prefixed with `can_` to avoid cross-file
# collisions.

# 1. Quebec Act Settlement -- the imperial accommodation of June 22nd

#    Coutume de Paris in property and civil matters, and replaced the

#    Council. American colonists denounced it as one of the Intolerable
