# 4. Shahna-i-Mandi -- the market superintendent established by Alauddin
#    Khalji circa 1296-1316, who fixed the price of grain, cloth, slaves,

#    physical mutilation. The reform fed a vast standing army by collapsing
#    soldier wages while keeping the rural producers' purchase price low,
#    but it depended on aggressive enforcement and ruined the merchants of
