# 1. Signoria delle Arti -- the Priori delle Arti, the executive of the
#    Florentine commune. The 1293 Ordinamenti di Giustizia of Giano della
#    Bella codified the Signoria of nine priors drawn by lot from the major
#    and minor guilds for two-month terms, and explicitly barred the
#    magnati (great magnate clans) from holding office. The institution
#    survived the Ciompi tumult, the Albizzi and Medici regimes, and was
