# National Destinies -- Latin Empire bureaucracies
# Five Crusader-state institutions inherited from the 1204 settlement and
# the Frankokratia governments of Greece. The Latin Empire was structurally
# Frankish-feudal, NOT Byzantine -- its bureaucracies should reflect knight
# service, baronial courts, papal-Latin clergy, and Italian commercial
# colonies, not court ceremonial or thematic governance.
#
# Auto-modifier grants 2 slots; capital adds 1 = 3 effective. Player picks 3 of 5.
#
# All IDs are lat_-prefixed to prevent collisions with vanilla BYZ bureaucracies
# and other modded tags' bureaucracies.

# 1. Partitio Romaniae -- the treaty signed 1 October 1204 dividing the
#    Byzantine Empire among the Fourth Crusade victors. The Doge of Venice
#    received three eighths and the lofty title Dominator quartae et dimidiae
#    partis totius Romaniae; the emperor a quarter; and the remaining
#    crusader barons divided the rest into hereditary fiefs of plena potestas
#    binding holders to annual military service from June 1 to September 29.
#    The partition entrenched a feudal aristocracy whose loyalty was
#    contractual, not personal, and left the throne structurally weak.
