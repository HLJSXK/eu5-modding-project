# 1. Sarbat Khalsa -- "the entire Khalsa," the biennial assembly of the
#    Sikh community summoned to Amritsar at Baisakhi and Diwali. Sitting
#    at the Akal Takht facing the Harmandir Sahib, the misl chiefs, the
#    granthis, and the Akali jathedars resolved disputes and issued
#    gurmatas -- decrees of the Guru Granth Sahib -- on war, treaty,
#    and excommunication. The institution sustained Sikh political unity
#    through the eighteenth-century period when no central state existed,
#    and it was the gurmata of 1733 that elected Kapur Singh leader of
#    the Dal Khalsa. Ranjit Singh quietly suspended it after 1805 because
#    its authority threatened his own.

# 2. Misl Confederacy -- the twelve cavalry confederacies (Sukerchakia,

#    Nakkai, Faizullapuria, Karor Singhia, Nishanwalia, Dallewalia)

# 4. Lahore Durbar -- Maharaja Ranjit Singh's central court, established

# 6. Daftar-i-Bahi & Jagirdari -- the revenue ledger system administered

#    permanent bahi (account-book). Sikh jagirs were attached to
