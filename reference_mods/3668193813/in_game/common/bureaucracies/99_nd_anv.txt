# National Destinies -- Angevin Empire bureaucracies

# privilege bargain that bought the Crown its Channel fleet.

# 1. Curia Regis Itinerant -- the king's household-court of justices,
#    constables, chamberlains and clerks that travelled with the
#    Plantagenet kings between Westminster, Rouen, Caen, Le Mans, Angers,
#    Chinon and Poitiers. As described by Walter Map and the Dialogus
#    de Scaccario of Richard FitzNeal (c. 1179), the curia handled fiscal
#    audit, royal pleas, foreign correspondence and dynastic business
#    interchangeably from wherever the king happened to lie. It was an
#    institution of motion, distinct from the later sedentary courts of
#    Common Pleas and King's Bench that branched off after 1234.

#    royal forest law administered by the King's foresters and verderers,

#    seal. The quarrel with Becket over the criminous-clerks article was

# 3. Cours Plenieres d'Aquitaine -- the great curia of the duke-king at
#    Poitiers and later Bordeaux, where the Aquitanian magnates -- the
#    counts of Angouleme, Perigord, La Marche, Armagnac and the lord of
#    Albret -- rendered counsel, heard appeals and confirmed the duchy's
#    customary law. Eleanor of Aquitaine sustained the Poitiers court
#    after 1168; Richard I, as count of Poitou, made it his political
#    centre; under the later Angevins the Bordeaux seneschal presided
#    in the king's absence. Unlike the writ-state of England, ducal

# 4. Cinque Ports Confederation -- the Plantagenet bargain with the
#    Channel naval towns of Hastings, New Romney, Hythe, Dover and
#    Sandwich (and the Antient Towns of Rye and Winchelsea). In return
#    for the Ship Service of 57 vessels for fifteen days per year, the
#    ports received sweeping liberties under the Charter of 1278: their
#    own admiralty court at Shepway, exemption from royal taxes and
#    customs, the right to send Barons to bear the canopy at coronations,
#    and effective immunity from the king's writ inside their walls.
#    The Crown got a standing fleet on the cheap; the ports got an
#    autonomy no other English borough enjoyed. The bargain held until
