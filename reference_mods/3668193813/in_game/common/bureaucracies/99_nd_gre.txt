# National Destinies -- Greek bureaucracies
# 6 historically-grounded institutions drawn from the layered Greek
# institutional inheritance: the Byzantine residue (Mount Athos), the
# Ottoman-era apparatus that governed the Rum (Patriarchate, Phanariots,
# kodjabashis, armatoloi-klepht pact), and the centralizing Kapodistrian
# secretariats of the first Hellenic Republic (1828-1831).
#
# Auto-modifier grants 2 slots; capital adds 1 = 3 total. Player picks 3 of 6.
#
# All IDs are gre_-prefixed to prevent collisions with other tags' bureaucracies
# (especially BYZ vanilla, which models the medieval Byzantine state -- a
# different institutional layer).
