# 1. Carta de Logu -- the 198-chapter legal code promulgated by Eleonora of
#    Arborea in 1392, written not in Latin but in the Sardinian vernacular
#    so it could be understood by those it governed. It granted women rights
#    to inherit and refuse marriage, mandated forest and waterway protections,
#    forbade torture without prior ill repute, and guaranteed the accused up
#    to ten witnesses for their defence. So effective that the Aragonese
#    conquerors adopted it wholesale; remained Sardinia's law until Charles

#    early 16th century the Stamenti met decennially under the viceroy at
