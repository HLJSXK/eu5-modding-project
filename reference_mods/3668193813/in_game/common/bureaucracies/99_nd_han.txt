# Scope note: HAN is a Level 2 formable, but Hanover's institutional history
# is unusually rich for a German territorial state -- the personal union
# with Britain (1714-1837) layered a dual-monarchy chancery on top of the

#    Regimentsordnung organised the ducal council into specialised chambers

#    personal union the Geheimer Rat became the de-facto government of the

# 2. Hannoversche Landstände -- the territorial estates of the Calenberg-

# 4. Deutsche Kanzlei in London -- the German Chancery, established by

#    elector-king's absence. The Kanzlei was a small office of three
#    Hanoverian ministers attached to the British court who transmitted
#    memoranda between the Geheimer Rat at Hanover and the elector, and
#    -- crucially -- kept Hanoverian foreign policy formally separate from
#    British. Friction between the Kanzlei's German ministers and the
