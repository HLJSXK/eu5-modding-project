# National Destinies -- Tabor Republic bureaucracies
# 4 historically-grounded institutions of the Hussite-Tabor commune. Auto-modifier
# grants 2 slots; the capital adds 1 = 3 total. Player picks 3 of 4.
#
# Identity: TAB inverts the medieval Bohemian estate hierarchy. The Hussite-Tabor
# commune is built on burgher town councils, peasant brotherhoods, and married
# vernacular clergy; hereditary nobles are structurally marginal or actively
# opposed. All four bureaucracies share that anti-feudal grain. Estate alignments
# privilege peasants / burghers / chalice clergy and disfavor nobles.

# 1. Hejtmane -- the four-captain elective executive of Tabor. Zizka pioneered
#    collective field command in 1420; after his death in 1424 the Sirotci and
#    Tabor wings each elected four captains (hejtmane) from city, parish, and
#    field-army constituencies. Apex republican-military authority of the
#    commune, modeled on the apostolic college rather than feudal kingship.

	implementation_price = price:implement_bureaucracy_price

#    Articles as constitutional law. The first sustained example in Latin
