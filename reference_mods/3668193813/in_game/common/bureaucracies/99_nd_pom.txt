# Scope rationale: 5 sits at the spec ceiling. POM's Level 2 status with
# institutional documentation across estates assemblies, ducal courts,

# Gutsherrschaft manorial system supports five distinct named institutions
# without filler. Choice ratio 2-of-5 gives strong tradeoff space across
# a contested borderland's competing power centres.

#    Landtag held veto power over ducal monetary levies and bound the
#    duke to consensus on every extraordinary expenditure. Its consent
#    legitimised ducal rule in a duchy chronically partitioned between

# 4. Bugenhagen Kirchenordnung -- the Lutheran church constitution drafted
#    by Doctor Pomeranus, Johannes Bugenhagen of Wollin, at the request of
#    Dukes Barnim XI and Philip I in 1534-1535 and finally implemented in
#    1563. It dissolved the Cammin chapter's old patrimony, transferred
#    the bishopric's revenues into ducal hands as a secular Stift, and
#    reorganised parish life under superintendents responsible for poor
