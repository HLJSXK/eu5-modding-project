# === TWITE TRIBUTARY ADMINISTRATION ===
# The Twite was the warlord-administrator of tributary subkingdoms (Kanyok, Kalundwe, Hemba),
# collecting copper ingots, ivory, and salt from the periphery and rendering them to the
# Mulopwe. Twite governance was indirect -- local chiefs kept their offices but accepted
# Luba supervisors (Reefe 1981). Effective for revenue, but built a quasi-noble class with
# its own retinues that later destabilised central authority.
