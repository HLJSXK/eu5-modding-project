# National Destinies -- Somali bureaucracies

# the medieval Islamic sultanates through the colonial-era resistance state.
# Auto-modifier grants 2 slots; capital adds 1 = 3 total. Player picks 3 of 6.

# 1. Ajuran Hydraulic Administration -- the Gareen rulers of the Ajuran imamate
#    (13th-17th c.) imposed the only hydraulic empire Africa would produce,
#    monopolizing the Shabelle and Jubba rivers through engineered wells, dikes,
#    and irrigation canals built by specialized Madinle diggers. The imam ruled
#    through wazirs, emirs, and naa'ibs, was the first to impose regular tribute
#    on Somali pastoralists and farmers, and minted bronze coins with his name
#    in Mogadishu's hinterland. The system survived in modified form into the
#    19th century; its taxation logic was inherited by every successor state.

# 2. Walashma Sultanate Court -- the Walashma dynasty ruled Ifat (1285-1415)
#    and its successor Adal (1415-1577) from the cities of Zeila and Harar,
#    the latter walled and minting its own coinage. The sultan was the
#    nominal Walashma sovereign drawn from a 13th-century lineage tracing to
#    Wali Asma; in practice the emirs governed the provinces and from 1471,
#    when Emir Laday Usman seized Dakkar, real power lay with whichever

# 3. Xeer and the Shir Beeleed -- the unwritten Somali customary law, passed
#    down orally and entered bilaterally between adjacent clans through their
#    elders. Disputes are resolved in the shir beeleed, the open-air clan
#    assembly where any adult male may speak and the xeer begti elders judge
#    by precedent. Compensation flows in mag (or diya), paid by the offender's
#    mag-paying lineage group to the victim's. The system requires no central
#    state, predates the sultanates, survives them all, and remains the de
#    facto law of much of the Somali interior into the modern day. The crown
#    cannot supersede it without breaking the legitimacy of the clans.

# 4. Sufi Tariqa Network -- the Qadiriyya, oldest and largest Sunni order in
#    Somalia from the 13th century onward, and the Salihiyya founded by Sayyid

# 5. Abbaan Caravan Brokerage -- the Somali caravan-protection institution
