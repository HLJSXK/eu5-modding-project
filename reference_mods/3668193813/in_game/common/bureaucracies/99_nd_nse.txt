# National Destinies -- North Sea Empire bureaucracies
# 7 historically-grounded institutions of Cnut the Great's Anglo-Scandinavian
# state, plausibly revivable as the federal apparatus of a thalassocratic
# revival linking the British Isles and Scandinavia. Auto-modifier grants
# 3 slots; capital adds 1 = 4 total. Player picks 4 of 7.
#
# Scope rationale: 7 sits inside the spec's 6-7 default. The Anglo-Danish
# state at its 1018-1035 peak documented institutions across central
# administration, military, fiscal, conciliar, legal, ecclesiastical, and
# monetary categories -- the catalog in Step 4's research turned up 8+
# named institutions before any cuts. Choice ratio 4-of-7 keeps each pick
# meaningful while reflecting the breadth of the historical apparatus.
#
# Candidate cuts: Things & lawspeakers (covered by existing nd_nse_thingstead
# building + nd_nse_lawspeaker_council production method, so a bureaucracy
# would duplicate hooks already in heritage advances).
