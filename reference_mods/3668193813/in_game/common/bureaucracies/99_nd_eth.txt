# 5. Gult and Rist Tenure -- the twin pillars of Ethiopian land law. Gult
#    was a fief-right, a non-hereditary grant from the negus or his governors
#    that conferred the right to collect tribute and labour from a defined
#    population; the holder was a fief-holder, not a landowner. Rist was the
#    inalienable hereditary use-right held by every descendant of an ancestral
#    first holder, distributed ambilineally across generations. Together they
