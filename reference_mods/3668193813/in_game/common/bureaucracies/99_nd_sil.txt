# Scope note: Silesia is Level 2 and a contested borderland province

# 1. Schlesischer Fürstentag -- The Silesian Princes' Diet, the
#    province-wide assembly meeting at Wrocław (Breslau). It convened
#    the dukes of the Silesian duchies (the Erbfürsten of Liegnitz,
#    Brieg, Münsterberg-Oels, Glogau, Sagan and others), the Prince-
#    Bishop of Wrocław, and the deputies of the six royal Silesian
#    cities. The Fürstentag granted taxes, raised the territorial levy,
#    and mediated disputes between the duchies. Compared to Bohemia or
#    Saxony, the Silesian estates were unusually fragmented but
#    correspondingly hard to coerce -- they treated the Bohemian king
#    as an external partner and survived as a real check on royal

# 2. Schlesisches Oberamt -- The Bohemian crown's chief office in
#    Silesia, headed by the Oberlandeshauptmann at Wrocław. The first
#    holder was Bishop Konrad of Oels, appointed 16 April 1422 by King
#    Sigismund to organize the defence against the Hussites; under
#    Matthias Corvinus the office became permanent from 1474. The
#    Oberamt outranked the territorial governors of the duchies,
#    exercised voluntary jurisdiction over the princes, and channelled
#    the Silesian tax-grant to Prague. After 1629 Ferdinand II
#    transformed it from a representative office into a purely
#    imperial bureaucracy, which is the form most useful to a
