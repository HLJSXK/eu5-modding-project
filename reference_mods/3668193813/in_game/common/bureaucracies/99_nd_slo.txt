# National Destinies -- Slovak (SLO) bureaucracies
# Six historically-grounded institutions of the Slovak lands (Upper
# Hungary / Felvidek). Auto-modifier grants 2 slots; capital adds 1 = 3
# total. Player picks 3 of 6.
#
# Identity boundary: this file models the institutional fabric of the
# Slovak territories, which were administered as part of the Hungarian
# Kingdom from 1000 to 1918 but retained distinctive Saxon-burgher,
# mining-town, and (later) national-revival institutions of their own.
# Where Slovakia and Hungary shared a structure (the comitatus), we
# model it from the Slovak county angle (the stolica assembly that
# Slovak speakers used the term for, and the Saxon-influenced upper
# counties of Spis, Sary, Liptov, and Orava).

# 1. Stolica / Varmegye -- the noble county. Inherited from the Arpadian
#    castle-counties, the comitatus was the basic unit of Hungarian
#    administration: a foispan (supremus comes) appointed by the king
#    presided over the stolica assembly of all enfranchised local
#    nobility, who elected a vice-foispan (alispan), the szolgabirok
#    judges, and the county's deputies to the Diet. In Slovak-speaking
#    counties the assembly was called stolica (the seat); each was a
#    little republic of the local nobility, jealous of royal interference.
