# National Destinies -- Rajputana bureaucracies
# 7 historically-grounded institutions of the Rajput confederation
# (Sisodiya Mewar, Kachhwaha Amber, Rathore Marwar, Bhati Jaisalmer,
# Hada Bundi/Kota, et al., 1336-1818).
# Auto-modifier grants 3 slots; capital adds 1 = 4 total. Player picks 4 of 7.
#
# All institutions are drawn from documented Rajput practice. The
# selection covers: clan-genealogical legitimacy (Bhat-Charan), the
# feudal subdivision that defined Rajput polity (Thikana/Pattedari),
# the classical privy council (Saptanga), clan-tutelary religion
# (Kuldevi shrines), the itinerant darbar (Hindola court), the
# Mughal-era compromise (Mansabdari), and the heroic-memory cult
# (Vijaya Stambha and the jauhar-saka complex at Chittor).

#    verifying gotra and sakha, and policed royal honor through the
#    threat of traga -- ritual self-mutilation that placed the king's

# 2. Thikana Pattedari Subdivision -- the Mewari and Marwari practice

#    imperial ranks and commanded Mughal armies while retaining
