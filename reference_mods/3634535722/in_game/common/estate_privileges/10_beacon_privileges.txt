# BEACON OF CIVILIZATION — Matter of Tork, Approach 2
#
# Two registers of co-optation, split by estate (decision 26):
#  AMIRS — the Turkic military elite transitions from de-facto
#  tribal aristocracy + military caste into PURE military
#  administration. Both privileges make the amirs HAPPIER while
#  dissolving them — historically the service elite walked into
#  Persianization willingly, one generation of sons at a time.
#  The cost is elsewhere: time, cabinet, the thinning cavalry.
#
#  TRIBES — Iranization of identity WITHOUT sedentarization (the
#  actual historical outcome: the lineage politics died, the
#  pastoralism didn't). Salaried pastoral levies here; the Īlāt
#  Compact reform (granted by the Iranization disposition's
#  completion) is the institutional capstone.
#
# Availability sticky via matter_tork_beacon_program. Granted
# privileges persist across approach switches (earned-gains rule).
#
# NOTE: the approach also REQUIRES amirs_umara_at_the_diwan
# (Amirs in the Cabinet) before the Iranization disposition can be
# claimed — to digest the amirs you must first seat them at the
# diwan. Niẓām al-Mulk's nightmare as the price of his solution.
# Its grant fires a one-shot −20 axis push toward Türk (hook in
# matter_tork_monthly_tick).
#
# Design: docs/design/matter_of_tork_design.md §4A, decisions 26-27.
# -----------------------------------------
