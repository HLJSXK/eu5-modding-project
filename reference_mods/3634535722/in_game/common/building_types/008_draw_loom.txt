# ============================================================
# ERANZAMIN — The naqshband chain: the Persian drawloom
#
# Naqshband = "pattern-binder": the master craftsman who ties
# the figure-harness of the drawloom (kargah-i naqshband),
# encoding the design into the loom's mechanics before a single
# weft is thrown. The Sufi shaykh Baha' al-Din Naqshband took
# his name from this trade. The drawloom is the technology
# behind Iranian zarbaf, kamkha, diba, and mukhmal — the
# brocade, lampas, velvet, and gold-thread silks of Yazd,
# Kashan, Tabriz, Mashhad, Mosul, and Herat. It is the
# silk-luxury infrastructure that fed Safavid silk diplomacy
# and supplied the Ottoman, Mughal, and European courts with
# the khil'a-grade textiles of the early modern Persianate.
#
# Two tiers — guild (1337) and workshop (1500–1600s Safavid
# expansion). No manufactory above: the naqshband craft does
# not industrialize in this period; the Safavid karkhana
# scale-up is handled as a base prod_efficiency modifier, not
# a separate building tier. The chain is Eranzamin-gated and
# sits parallel to the base fine_cloth chain — a player inside
# Eranzamin can build both, with the naqshband route taking
# silk and turning it into fine_cloth at a drawloom premium.
#
# The figure-harness craft is centuries old here (Sasanian in
# its first form, Abbasid in its tiraz workshops, Saljuq in
# its brocade industry), and Mustawfi names Yazdi brocade
# explicitly in the Nuzhat al-Qulub (c. 1340). It is the
# baseline at start, not a tech to unlock.
# ============================================================
