#############################################################
####### National Ideas
#############################################################

- Added new systems: Dynamic and Historic National Ideas
- Historic National Ideas allow you to pick unique bonuses depending on your country if the country has a unique idea set
- Dynamic National Ideas allow you to pick dynamic national ideas for your country
- Added a Game Option where you can decide between Dynamic National Ideas, Historic National Ideas and deactivating the system
- Added Game Option where you can choose how many National Ideas can be picked (For historic sets there might not be more than 10 supported)
- In case Historic National Ideas are chosen and your country does not have a unique ideaset, dynamic ideas are used
- The initial picking of National Ideas requires "Progress" in the respective category
- Picking Ideas of one category will increase the cost of picking more ideas from that category
- National Ideas are usually upgradeable, which costs innovation points
- Upgrading National Ideas will also increase the cost of upgrading more ideas from that category
- Changing your tag from a tag with a unique historical idea set to a tag with an own unique set will enable both Historical Idea sets
- Forming a tag with a unique idea set will enable that idea set, but you will retain your picked dynamic ideas
