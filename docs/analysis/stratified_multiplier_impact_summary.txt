INTERPRETATION:
Per-stratum savings pressure adjustments make the target vector MORE sensitive to estate
gold imbalances. When one stratum's gold/target ratio diverges from others', the stratified
target direction rotates away from the raw baseline's equilibrium, often exiting the cone
of feasible solutions. The unified multiplier acts as a stabilizer: it scales all targets
proportionally, preserving the direction that raw spending naturally aligns to.

RECOMMENDATION:
Keep the unified multiplier. Stratified adjustments would require re-tuning capacity targets
or adding a total-preservation constraint to compensate for the ~60% drop in exact-solve
success rate, which would complicate the solver without delivering a clear gameplay benefit.
