[h1]SOL-PP Compatibility Submod[/h1]

This is a required compatibility patch for [b]Standard of Living (SOL)[/b] and [b]Prosper or Perish (PP)[/b]. It is not standalone.

[h2]What It Does[/h2]

[list]
[*]SOL and PP both change pop demand, food, climate, roads, RGO values, age scaling, and war-related modifiers. Without a patch, some changes overwrite each other while others stack unexpectedly.
[*]PP also adds victuals and removes lumber pop demand, but normal SOL calculations do not handle those changes correctly.
[*]With this patch, [b]full PP leads the shared economy, food, infrastructure, and location balance[/b].
[*]SOL keeps its core income-based dynamic demand, Living Standard UI and map, full victuals support, zero lumber demand, and a small selection of non-conflicting economic and war rules.
[/list]
