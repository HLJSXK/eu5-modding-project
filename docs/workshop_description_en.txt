[h2]October 2026 Major Update[/h2]
The biggest update since the 1.4 compatibility patch. [b]A new campaign is strongly recommended.[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Goods substitution is back.[/b] 56 goods in 20 substitute groups (staples, protein, clothing, heating, drinks, luxuries, mounts...). Each estate consumes whole groups. Seven price tiers move demand from expensive goods to cheaper ones in the same group. Applied per country as "Goods substitution" modifiers; can be turned off in CMF.
[*][b]Living standard is more than money.[/b] It is now separate from purchasing power, which still alone decides what pops buy. Hospitals, lazarettos, pest houses and aqueducts raise it; tanneries, putrefaction works and iron/steel mills lower it; theaters, opera houses and libraries raise it by a percentage. Higher living standard attracts migrants; very low living standard slows population growth, high living standard speeds it up.
[*][b]Brand-new SOL interface.[/b] A four-section bar below the country buttons: living standard, purchasing power, goods substitution and world ranking. Hover to preview a map mode, click to open it. Four new map modes, substitute groups in goods tooltips, new icons, Glorp UI compatible.
[*]The Living Standard situation is removed; the new bar covers everything it showed.
[*]Baseline baskets recalibrated to 1340 country medians. AI recalculates its economy yearly by default (players monthly).
[*]SOL Standalone and all compatibility submods are updated too.
[/list]

[h2]Overview[/h2]
Standard of Living (SOL) is an economy and balance overhaul for deep-strategy players. Pop demand follows what each estate can actually afford, goods substitute for each other by price, and living standard counts the things money cannot buy.

[h2]Core Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Income-based pop demand[/b]: Each month, every estate's local income, building upkeep and national savings are compared with the cost of a calibrated basket of 56 goods. This purchasing power scales its demand. Development no longer scales or gates demand.
[*][b]Goods substitution[/b]: 20 substitute groups. Each estate consumes whole groups and shifts spending from expensive goods to cheaper ones in the same group.
[*][b]Living standard[/b]: Money plus buildings such as hospitals, aqueducts, polluting workshops and theaters. It affects migration attraction and population growth.
[*][b]SOL interface[/b]: A four-section bar below the country buttons, four map modes, substitute groups in goods tooltips, and a SOL button in the location window.
[/list]

[h2]Economic Balance[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Age economic escalation[/b]: As the game advances through the ages, building efficiency falls, RGO expansion becomes more expensive, city/town upgrades cost more, and pop food consumption rises. Later ages regain prosperity, but expansion becomes much more costly.
[*][b]Anti-snowball construction[/b]: Base RGO size is reduced, low-control locations are worse for construction, and roads/railroads are much more expensive.
[*][b]Tax and difficulty tuning[/b]: Default base tax efficiency is -15% and can be adjusted through CMF. Vanilla tax bonuses from AI Hard/Very Hard and Player Easy/Very Easy are halved.
[*][b]Diplomacy and stability investment[/b]: Diplomatic spending changes with used diplomatic capacity, while cultural tradition can reduce stability investment cost.
[*][b]GDP-to-development[/b]: Locations receive yearly development growth scaled by local GDP.
[/list]

[h2]War And Expansion[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Harsher war consequences[/b]: War exhaustion further damages levy size, control, stability, legitimacy, and defensive capability.
[*][b]Occupation pressure[/b]: Total occupation and capital occupation greatly accelerate war exhaustion.
[*][b]Wartime restriction[/b]: Optionally prevents the Reduce War Exhaustion cabinet action while at war.
[*][b]Local war damage[/b]: Blockades, sieges, occupations, looting, horde raids, and razing more strongly affect prosperity, control, construction, migration attraction, development, raw materials, and food.
[*][b]Colonial restrictions[/b]: AI generally needs more than 500 tax base to create colonial charters. Colonial nations only colonize in their capital region. Historical colonizers are exempt. Players are not restricted.
[/list]

[h2]Food, Climate, And Prices[/h2]
[list]
[*]Normal winters and raised levies create stronger local food pressure.
[*]Extreme Little Ice Age event penalties to food and output are softened to reduce pure disaster spirals.
[*]Many gold transfers, advisors, commanders, religious actions, diplomatic actions, and road prices are rebalanced. Actions involving generals, admirals, and advisors now cost gold.
[/list]

[h2]CMF Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Use [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3692202776]Community Mod Framework (CMF)[/url] 2.x to control this mod's features in game.[/b]
[*]SOL pop demand master toggle, map coloring toggle and goods substitution toggle.
[*]Economic balance master toggle, base tax efficiency slider, age escalation, stability investment discount, diplomatic spending adjustment, difficulty tax nerf, GDP-to-development, and AI prosperity recovery.
[*]War acceleration master toggle, higher war exhaustion, harsher war exhaustion effects, and wartime Reduce War Exhaustion restriction.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]New campaigns are recommended. Older saves load, but caches and market prices need time to settle.
[*]Prosper or Perish, MEIOU and Taxes and Just Goods need their SOL compatibility submod, loaded below both main mods. See the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3769554515]Compatibility Submod Collection[/url].
[*]Other mods that change pop demand or goods are incompatible unless a submod exists. Feel free to request one; making it is not difficult.
[*]SOL ships a location window based on [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3812518640]Glorp UI[/url] with the SOL button inserted. Glorp UI and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3736668860]Construction Manager[/url] are optional. Other mods that override the whole location window conflict.
[/list]
