[h1]SOL-JTG Compatibility Submod[/h1]

This is a required compatibility patch for [b]Standard of Living (SOL)[/b] and [b]Just Goods[/b]. It is not standalone.

[h2]What It Does[/h2]

[list]
[*]Just Goods adds new goods with its own fixed pop demand, while SOL uses a dynamic income-based demand system and does not normally include those goods in its calculations or UI.
[*]With this patch, Just Goods still provides the new goods and production content, while [b]SOL takes over the population demand of the 30 new demand goods[/b].
[*]The new goods are included in SOL's demand calculations and goods panels. Slave population demand for these added goods is zero.
[*]Wealth, development, and regional demand gates on those direct pop-demand goods are neutralized so SOL's income-driven demand remains consistent across locations.
[*]Updated for EU5 1.4 and Just Goods 1.1, including pop demand for obsidian, jade, plaster, and diamonds; goods panels display 86 goods in total.
[*]All 30 direct-demand Just Goods goods now join SOL substitute groups: all 86 goods share price-based substitution, seven-level goods markers, contextual tooltips and the six-slot HUD. The 12 demand-free goods remain excluded.
[*]Validation targets new campaigns; existing-save compatibility has not been verified.
[/list]
