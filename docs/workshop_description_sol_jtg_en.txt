[h1]SOL-JTG Compatibility Submod[/h1]

This is a required compatibility patch for [b]Standard of Living (SOL)[/b] and the five [b]Just Trade Goods (JTG)[/b] mods. It is not standalone.

[h2]What It Does[/h2]

[list]
[*]JTG adds new goods with its own fixed pop demand, while SOL uses a dynamic income-based demand system and does not normally include those goods in its calculations or UI.
[*]With this patch, JTG still provides the new goods and production content, while [b]SOL takes over the population demand of the 22 new demand goods[/b].
[*]The new goods are included in SOL's demand calculations and goods panels. JTG slave demand remains unchanged.
[/list]

[h2]Required Mods[/h2]

[b]All five JTG mods are required.[/b]

[h2]Load Order[/h2]

[olist]
[*]Community Mod Framework
[*]The five JTG mods (any order)
[*]Standard of Living
[*][b]SOL-JTG Compatibility Submod[/b]
[/olist]

[b]This patch must be below SOL and all five JTG mods.[/b]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

Made for SOL 1.3.11 and the current versions of the five linked JTG mods. A new campaign is recommended.

Do not use this together with the SOL-PP compatibility patch. A PP + JTG setup requires a separate merged patch.
