===== 中文评论（Steam 评论上限 1000 字符，以下约 300 字） =====

【大版本更新】自 1.4 兼容更新以来最大的一次更新，强烈建议开新档：
1. 替代组回来了：56 种商品分成 20 个替代组，某种商品贵了，人们会转买同组里便宜的替代品。
2. 生活水平不再只是钱：医院、引水渠、剧院等建筑提高生活水平，污染工厂降低生活水平；生活水平会影响移民吸引力和人口增长。
3. 全新 SOL 界面：国家按钮下方的四格信息栏 + 四个地图模式，悬停预览、点击切换。“生活水平”局势已移除，信息全部移到信息栏。
SOL Standalone 和所有兼容子模组已同步更新。完整说明见介绍页顶部，遇到问题欢迎在这里留言，或到 Discord / QQ 群（1070041687）反馈！

===== English comment (about 650 characters) =====

[Major update] The biggest update since the 1.4 compatibility patch. A new campaign is strongly recommended.
1. Goods substitution is back: 56 goods in 20 substitute groups. When a good gets expensive, people switch to cheaper goods in the same group.
2. Living standard is more than money: hospitals, aqueducts and theaters raise it, polluting workshops lower it, and it now affects migration attraction and population growth.
3. Brand-new SOL interface: a four-section bar below the country buttons plus four map modes; hover to preview, click to open. The Living Standard situation is gone; everything it showed now lives in the bar.
SOL Standalone and all compatibility submods are updated too. Full notes are at the top of the description. Please report issues here or on Discord!
