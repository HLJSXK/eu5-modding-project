## Standard of Living — October 2026 Major Update

The biggest SOL update since the 1.4 compatibility patch is live! **A new campaign is strongly recommended.**

**1. Goods substitution is back**
In EU5 1.1, SOL had substitute groups, until a 1.2 engine change broke the interface they ran on. They are now rebuilt on a new, much lighter foundation.
- 56 goods in 20 substitute groups: staples, protein, clothing, heating, drinks, luxuries, mounts and more.
- Each estate consumes whole groups. Seven price tiers move demand from expensive goods to cheaper ones in the same group.
- Shown as "Goods substitution (good)" country modifiers. Can be turned off in CMF.

**2. Living standard is more than money**
- Living standard is now separate from purchasing power, which still alone decides how much pops buy.
- Hospitals, lazarettos, pest houses and aqueducts raise it. Tanneries, putrefaction works and iron/steel mills lower it. Theaters, opera houses and libraries raise it by a percentage.
- Higher living standard attracts migrants. Very low living standard slows population growth; high living standard speeds it up.

**3. Brand-new SOL interface**
- A four-section bar under the country buttons: living standard, purchasing power, goods substitution, world ranking. Hover to preview a map, click to open it.
- Four new map modes, substitute groups in goods tooltips, new icons, Glorp UI compatible.
- The Living Standard situation is removed; the bar covers everything it showed.

**Also:** baskets recalibrated to 1340 medians; AI recalculates yearly by default (CMF Performance tab). SOL Standalone and all compatibility submods (PP, M&T, Just Goods) are updated too.
